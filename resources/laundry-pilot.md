We are happy to announce that, funded and organized by individual dorm
governments, DormCon, and the UA, **beginning September 1st, 2026 and ending May
31st, 2027, laundry machines in all undergraduate residence halls will be
free-to-use**. This means that all machines can be used as often as needed, and
you will no longer need to use the CSC GO app to begin a cycle.

<!-- truncate -->

DormCon began working with Housing and Residential Services (HRS) to initiate
these pilots after hearing from residents how temporary free-to-use laundry at
various different instances has been beneficial to dorms and their communities.

We then began an ongoing laundry pilot in Simmons and Baker, where all washers
and dryers in those dorms are free-to-use. Organized by DormCon and funded by
individual dorm governments, these pilots sought to understand how water usage,
energy consumption, and maintenance costs change when laundry machines can be
freely used over an academic year.

Over the course of academic year 2025-2026, the data we’ve gathered from Simmons
and Baker has been extremely promising so far. Thus, based on existing feedback
and current pilot data, we have worked to expand this pilot campus-wide in the
upcoming academic year. We'll continue working with HRS to monitor program
impacts and explore implementing free-to-use laundry permanently.

Your [dorm presidents](../about/voting-members),
[DormCon Exec](../about/officers), and the
[UA Officers](https://ua.mit.edu/officers/) have worked with HRS over the course
of the academic year to make this possible. If you have any thoughts, comments,
or concerns, please don’t hesitate to reach out to <dormcon-laundry@mit.edu>.
